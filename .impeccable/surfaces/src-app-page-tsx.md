# Surface brief: landing page (`/`)

**Mode:** Persuade.

**Visitor and job:** startups and founders, local businesses, offices and institutions, and overseas clients judging whether Dzypher can build their web and mobile product, then getting in touch.

**Action:** start a project by email; the enquiry composer lets visitors tick what they need and prefills the email.

**Proof:** the Suki platform (3 Kotlin Multiplatform apps + 3 Next.js apps on one Supabase backend), the PCSD Document Tracking System, and Taga-Cuyo (Best Capstone Project Paper, PSU 2025).

**Constraints:** no invented testimonials, client logos, or metrics. Suki screens are coded recreations from the real apps (labelled sample data); PCSD is a workflow animation, never a fake screen. Sakayna is excluded. Every animation has a reduced-motion path and pauses offscreen.

**History:** the first build (category standard) was rejected by the owner the same day for having no animation and no unique design; the owner then chose The Cut from three overdrive directions.

**Memorable moment:** the logo made of particles splits along its Z cut, and the phone + dashboard demo plays the order sync as you scroll.

**Unresolved:** real Suki and PCSD screenshots; an Open Graph image.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The Dzypher mark is a D cut in two by a Z: two halves, web and mobile, that fit as one. The whole page is built from that cut. It refuses the static, card-grid studio page the owner rejected.

OWN-WORLD: Ink `#0e1117` ground (the logo's own), paper `#f5f7fa` and teal `#2dd4bf` as the two halves. Section boundaries are Z-steps at the logo's 47° angle. Archivo pushed to its extremes (width 118–125, weight 850) for display, stretching from condensed into place; floating glass pill nav; teal used as fields, not just accents.

STORY: Visitors watch the mark assemble, see it split to reveal a live order flowing from phone to dashboard and back, explore what Dzypher builds through animated tiles, scroll through three real products, then email a brief.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-height ink. Left: an uppercase extended headline over four lines that stretches in line by line, a two-line sub, and a magnetic teal "Start a project" pill plus a ghost link. Right: the particle mark (paper upper half, teal lower half) that scatters around the cursor. Bottom edge: a moving stack marquee.

FORM: The Cut, overdrive direction 1 of 3, chosen by the owner after rejecting the canon build; seed:826efb663fe34fee.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
